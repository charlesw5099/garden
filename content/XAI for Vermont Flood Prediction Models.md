# The Case for Explainable AI

When it comes down to it, even if results from the implementation of AI models prove to be promising, that is still only one aspect of multiple that needs to be considered when it comes to widespread adoption of the technology. Transparency in how these models come to their decisions is especially important when making a case for their use. How can we trust their decisions in such serious cases when we cannot even fully interpret their internal workings that lead them towards them? This is why some refer to using the term _black box_ when it comes to describing their decision-making.

For policymakers and agencies to consider adoption, it's a completely fair point to question. This is primarily the reason why looking to comprehensible, explainable AI is important for moving forward with change. We need to be able to have some insight towards why we see the results that we do.
